💡 Energy Conservation Measures (ECMs)

Structured standard operating procedures for reducing energy usage, carbon footprint, and operational cost.

💡 1. Lighting System Retrofits
  • LED Conversion: Replace fluorescent tube fittings with high-efficiency LED lights (50% energy reduction).
  • Daylight Harvesting: Utilize natural lighting in high-window corridors and library spaces.
❄️ 2. HVAC & Thermal Management
  • Variable Speed Drives (VFDs): Retrofit large extractor fans and air handling units in science laboratories.
  • Natural Ventilation: Optimize window operation in hostels and staff offices.
⚙️ 3. Motors, Pumps & Controls
  • Motor and Pump Maintenance: Check alignment, bearings, filters, leaks, and operating schedules.
  • Smart Power Strips: Cut phantom/vampire loads from electronics and laboratory equipment overnight.
☀️ 4. Renewable Energy Integration
  • Solar PV Roof Microgrids: Install solar roof panels on academic complexes for day-time baseload generation.
  • Solar Thermal Water Heating: Replace electric immersion heaters in student hostels with solar water heaters.
